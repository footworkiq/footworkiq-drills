# Decision Card Audit, November 2026

This audit covers all 72 decision cards in `cards/decision/`: 32 attack, 24 defend and 16 mindset. Each card is checked for answer-length cues (the best answer giving itself away by being the longest), the stored order of its answers, cards that are too obvious, contested gradings, its age band, its role, its difficulty and its tags. Every card PR in the audit links to this file and adds its rows to the per-card table.

## Review status

These cards are author-reviewed, not coach-reviewed. No qualified coach has reviewed the deck yet, so every card's `reviewed_by` is empty.

## Method

- Option texts are rebalanced in length from both directions: long best answers are tightened and short wrong answers are given more detail. The meaning of each option stays the same.
- The stored order of each card's options comes from the sha256 hash of the card id, used to pick one of six fixed orders. `node scripts/reorder-card-options.js` applies it, so the best answer isn't always first. The same id always gives the same order.
- On each best/poor/poor card, one poor answer is rewritten into a tempting answer graded ok, so the card asks for a real choice.
- `rev` goes to 2 only when a grade or the situation on the card changes. Rewording alone keeps `rev` at 1.
- The numbers in this file come from `node scripts/card-report.js`.

## Age band and role rulings

- **a31 `header-chance`: `age_band` set to `U16`.** England youth football phases heading in from U12, and none is allowed in training or matches before then. The card keeps its heading content but only shows to the oldest band.
- **a28 `offside`: `age_band` set to `U12`.** There is no offside in mini-soccer. It starts at 9v9. The tip keeps its note that many junior leagues don't use offside yet.
- **d6 `keeper-one-on-one`, d14 `high-cross` and d22 `back-pass`: `role` set to `keeper`.** These cards are written from the keeper's point of view. Outfield profiles won't see them. Keeper profiles see them along with every outfield card. Their situations are unchanged; only their options are length-balanced, so they stay `rev` 1.

**Other rulings.** The attack, defend and mindset PRs add any further age band, role, laws or grading rulings here as they find them.

- **a14 `keeper-off-the-line`: "Blast it low at goal" regraded poor → ok (ruled 2026-11-06).** A shot on target is never a bad idea, so poor was too harsh. The chip stays best because the keeper is out in the path of a low shot. `rev` 2.
- **a15 `throw-in`: `age_band` set to `U12` (ruled 2026-11-06).** Mini-soccer (U7 to U11) restarts with a pass-in or dribble-in, not a throw-in. Rejected alternative: keep `all` and reword the card for either restart.
- **a19 `winning-one-minute-left`: development note added to the tip (ruled 2026-11-06).** Shielding by the corner flag stays best. The tip now says that at youth level going for another goal is fine too.
- **a20 `weaker-foot`: "Take a touch onto your stronger foot" regraded poor → ok (ruled 2026-11-06).** The touch gives a cleaner strike but moves you towards the defender, so it is a trade-off, not a mistake. Shooting with the weaker foot stays best. `rev` 2.
- **a21 `goal-kick`: situation reworded away from "You're the keeper" (ruled 2026-11-06).** The card now says "You're taking a goal kick", so it suits outfield players, who take goal kicks in many junior games. It also has the other team's forwards waiting on halfway, which fits the mini-soccer retreat line. `role` stays `outfield`. `rev` 2 because the situation changed. Rejected alternative: keep the keeper wording and set `role: keeper`.
- **d1 `last-defender`, d3 `runner-on-your-blind-side`, d8 `beaten`: one poor answer rewritten as a tempting ok (ruled 2026-11-06).** d1: "Drop back towards the edge of your box" (backing off buys time, but too far gives them a shot). d3: "Close in to help your teammate win it" (doubling up might win it, but frees the runner). d8: "Stop and shout for a teammate to cover" (talking helps, but stopping takes you out of the game). The best answer is unchanged on all three. `rev` 2.
- **d19 `hold-the-line`: `age_band` set to `U12` (ruled 2026-11-06).** The best answer, stepping up to catch the striker offside, only works where offside applies, and there is no offside in mini-soccer. Rejected alternative: keep `all` and rely on the tip's "Check whether your league uses offside".
- **d12 `their-throw-in`: situation reworded as "throw-in or pass-in" (ruled 2026-11-06).** The title and text now cover both restarts, so the card stays `all`. `rev` 2 because the situation changed. Rejected alternative: keep the throw-in text and set `age_band` to `U12` (`rev` 1).
- **d17 `their-keepers-ball`: no change beyond rebalance and metadata (ruled 2026-11-06).** The card describes pressing once the keeper plays it out. In mini-soccer the other team must retreat at goal kicks and when the keeper has the ball in hand, so the poor answer ("Walk back to the halfway line") is what the laws require at those moments. The risk is low because the card is about the pass to a defender, not the restart. Rejected alternatives: add a tip note about waiting until the ball is in play, or set `age_band` to `U12`.
- **d9 `corner-against-you` and d14 `high-cross`: header wording removed from two whys (ruled 2026-11-06).** "score a header" and "a free header" became "a free shot at goal" and "a free go at the ball", because heading is phased in from U12 and both cards show to every age. Grades and situations are unchanged. `rev` 1.
- **m10 `player-down`: reworded for youth football (ruled 2026-11-06).** The text now says play is still going, and the best answer reads "Stop and tell the referee, or kick it out". It is phrased as an action, not a rule: Law 5 has the referee stop play for a serious injury, but no FA text says youth referees stop play for every knock. The best option id and grade are unchanged; `rev` 2 because the situation changed. Rejected alternative: keep the old best text ("Kick the ball out so they can get help") and only reword the situation.
- **m1, m4, m7, m8, m11, m13, m15: one poor answer rewritten as a tempting ok (ruled 2026-11-06).** m1 "Drop back into your position to defend"; m4 "Ask the referee calmly what it was for"; m7 "Calmly ask the ref to watch that player"; m8 "Skip the celebration and run straight back"; m11 "Go over everything you have to do in your head"; m13 "Go and say sorry to your keeper and team"; m15 "Kick it long so it doesn't get stuck". Each why credits what the answer gets right. The best answer is unchanged on all seven. `rev` 2. The frozen option ids on these cards (for example `argue-referee`) keep their old names, as with d8, because answer history is stored by id. Rejected alternatives: for m4, "Shrug it off but stay annoyed about it"; rewriting the other poor option on each card instead.
- **m7 `hard-foul` and m12 `winning-easily`: checked against the fair-play rules.** Neither card has retaliation, showing off or unsafe play as best. No change beyond rebalance and metadata.
