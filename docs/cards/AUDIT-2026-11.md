# Decision Card Audit, November 2026

This audit covers all 72 decision cards in `cards/decision/`: 32 attack, 24 defend and 16 mindset. Each card is checked for answer-length cues (the best answer giving itself away by being the longest), the stored order of its answers, cards that are too obvious, contested gradings, its age band, its role, its difficulty and its tags. Every card PR in the audit links to this file and adds its rows to the per-card table.

## Review status

These cards are author-reviewed, not coach-reviewed. No qualified coach has reviewed the deck yet, so every card's `reviewed_by` is empty.

## Method

- Option texts are rebalanced in length from both directions: long best answers are tightened and short wrong answers are given more detail. The meaning of each option stays the same.
- The stored order of each card's options comes from the sha256 hash of the card id, used to pick one of six fixed orders. `node scripts/reorder-card-options.js` applies it, so the best answer isn't always first. The same id always gives the same order.
- On each best/poor/poor card, one poor answer is rewritten into a tempting answer graded ok, so the card asks for a real choice.
- `rev` goes to 2 only when a grade or the situation on the card changes. Rewording alone keeps `rev` at 1.
- The numbers in this file come from `node scripts/card-report.js`.

## Age band and role rulings

- **a31 `header-chance`: `age_band` set to `U16`.** England youth football phases heading in from U12, and none is allowed in training or matches before then. The card keeps its heading content but only shows to the oldest band.
- **a28 `offside`: `age_band` set to `U12`.** There is no offside in mini-soccer. It starts at 9v9. The tip keeps its note that many junior leagues don't use offside yet.
- **d6 `keeper-one-on-one`, d14 `high-cross` and d22 `back-pass`: `role` set to `keeper`.** These cards are written from the keeper's point of view. Outfield profiles won't see them. Keeper profiles see them along with every outfield card.

**Other rulings.** The attack, defend and mindset PRs add any further age band, role, laws or grading rulings here as they find them.

- **a14 `keeper-off-the-line`: "Blast it low at goal" regraded poor → ok (ruled 2026-11-06).** A shot on target is never a bad idea, so poor was too harsh. The chip stays best because the keeper is out in the path of a low shot. `rev` 2.
- **a15 `throw-in`: `age_band` set to `U12` (ruled 2026-11-06).** Mini-soccer (U7 to U11) restarts with a pass-in or dribble-in, not a throw-in. Rejected alternative: keep `all` and reword the card for either restart.
- **a19 `winning-one-minute-left`: development note added to the tip (ruled 2026-11-06).** Shielding by the corner flag stays best. The tip now says that at youth level going for another goal is fine too.
- **a20 `weaker-foot`: "Take a touch onto your stronger foot" regraded poor → ok (ruled 2026-11-06).** The touch gives a cleaner strike but moves you towards the defender, so it is a trade-off, not a mistake. Shooting with the weaker foot stays best. `rev` 2.
- **a21 `goal-kick`: situation reworded away from "You're the keeper" (ruled 2026-11-06).** The card now says "You're taking a goal kick", so it suits outfield players, who take goal kicks in many junior games. It also has the other team's forwards waiting on halfway, which fits the mini-soccer retreat line. `role` stays `outfield`. `rev` 2 because the situation changed. Rejected alternative: keep the keeper wording and set `role: keeper`.
